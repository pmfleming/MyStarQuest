# Royal Teenieping difficulty symbol

The Teenie Friends difficulty selectors use the Royal Teenieping gold crown emblem with curved side arches: one for the first level and two for the second, through the `difficultyIcon` role. All maths games also use this emblem as their shared `mathsCounter`, including arithmetic counters and the tens rods and individual ones in large-number exercises.

The Hundreds, Tens and Ones place-value columns also use the emblem through `placeValueCounter`: a 10 × 10 repeated grid for each hundred, ten emblems per tens rod, and individual emblems for ones.
