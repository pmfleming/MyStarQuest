Notes that affect guessing rounds

  • Bellaping: Shrinks objects; Ellaping enlarges them. The sisters share a harmony theme. Source
  • Chocoping: Her hair itself is listed as her magical prop; do not invent a handheld chocolate tool. Source
  • Cupping: The pacifier-based baby spell is shared with twin Muffping, who has no portrait in this folder. Source
  • Cureping: Selected ability is summoning helpers; ordinary medical supplies in her kit are not all magical. Source
  • Dadaping: The current wiki uses 'rightness'; it notes older use of 'honesty'. 'Doing things right' avoids conflating him with Trueping. Source
  • Ellaping: Enlarges objects; Bellaping shrinks them. The sisters share a harmony theme. Source
  • Flitterping: Her wings themselves are her prop; this is not a missing handheld item. Source
  • Frogping: Frogping is revealed as a form of Henryping. Retain both asset rows, but avoid treating them as two unrelated characters. Source
  • Giggleping: The transformed, mischievous counterpart of Happying; retain both pictures as distinct forms, not distinct people. Source
  • Graceping: Theme is a child-facing description of her graceful movement, not a claim about a fixed English translation of her concept. Source
  • Happying: Same underlying character as Giggleping. This picture is Happying's regular royal design. Source
  • Heartsping: Use the season-one mirror design shown in this asset. Later seasons use different props and powers. Source
  • Henryping: Same character as Frogping in a different form; avoid placing both forms together in beginner rounds. Source
  • Hopping: Sources vary between 'mettle' and 'overcoming'; 'Keep trying' is the editorial child-facing theme. Source
  • Kissyping: The stroller is listed as her prop, but her healing magic is performed by blowing kisses. Source
  • Onlyping: This portrait matches the earlier hair-stick design; later appearances use a staff. Onlyping also appears disguised as Maskping. Source
  • Romi (Romiping form): Human Romi temporarily transformed into a Teenieping in season 5, not an independent emotion fairy. MAGIC describes what happened to her, not a verified innate power. Keep as an optional bonus character. No dedicated prop or innate magic for this temporary form was established by the consulted sources; do not reuse human Romi's catching tools as if they were this form's prop. Source
  • Shimmerping: Her theme is healing, but her flute also makes ice. The ice clue is intentional and supported by the character page. Source
  • Sleeping: The wiki uses Sleeping, also known as Sleepyping; the filename is 'sleep'. Source
  • Sneakyping: Simplified description of the wiki's removal of an evil self; do not interpret this as stealing ordinary belongings. Source
  • Snuggyping: Filename 'snuggle' maps to the wiki's Snuggyping. The sheep helpers and laundry equipment may need separate clue artwork. Source
  • Sweetping: This selected power requires Sweetping and Tangyping together; it is not exclusive to Sweetping. Source
  • Tangyping: This selected power requires Tangyping and Sweetping together; it is not exclusive to Tangyping. Source
