# Princess chore artwork

All four revised chore images are installed and referenced by the project. They were redrawn with the built-in image generation tool, using Write and Tidy as the original style, character, and three-quarter framing references. Water and Dinner use the completed Brave image as their final character reference.

- Brave: `src/assets/themes/princess/brave-princess.png` — close crown-to-hips view, confident posture, simple pink-and-gold heart shield.
- Getting dressed: `src/assets/themes/princess/getting-dressed-quickly.png` — close crown-to-hips view, fully dressed princess pulling a cardigan sleeve into place, small alarm clock.
- Water: `src/assets/themes/princess/water-toilet-check-overview.webp` — princess holding a blue water bottle, with a small pink-and-white toilet.
- Dinner: `src/assets/themes/princess/dinner-overview.webp` — princess eating vegetables at a compact wooden table.

All four production images are 768 × 768 with verified transparent alpha (0–255). Transparency was produced by the built-in image tool and preserved during resizing; Water and Dinner use lossless WebP. Write and Tidy are unchanged.

The generation and final correction prompts are recorded in [chore-redraw-prompts.json](chore-redraw-prompts.json).

The revised Settings and Delete icons are installed as `edit.svg` and `delete.svg` in the same theme directory. These native vector assets use transparent backgrounds, matching rose-and-gold palettes, and balanced artwork bounds. The princess Delete button also uses coordinated rose styling and hover colors.
