# Bad Dinosaurs collection completeness check

Checked 16 September 2026 against the user's supplied screenshots, the indexed [Dinopedia creature list](https://dinopedia.fandom.com/wiki/Bad_Dinosaurs), and the actual profiles in `src/data/dinosaurKnowledge.ts`.

The initial audit found **16 checklist entries**, with **10 implemented and six absent**. The subsequent addition implements Allosaurus, Baculites, Dimetrodon, Otodus megalodon and Tetrapodophis. The current game has **15 creatures**; **Donnie is intentionally excluded** at the user's request. The two supplied screenshots show the same list. Tyrannosaurus is already represented by Tyrannosaurus rex; it is not another missing entry.

Dinopedia is a fan-maintained checklist, not an official production species register. Matching its list establishes these omissions relative to the requested checklist; it does not independently confirm every cartoon-to-species identification or every episode appearance. Keep uncertain matches qualified.

| Initial omission | Checklist qualification                     | Educational handling                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Dimetrodon       | Listed without a pilot-only qualifier       | A sail-backed non-mammalian synapsid from the Permian, not a dinosaur. Its sail's precise function should not be taught as settled. [Harvard Museum of Natural History](https://whatsinaname.hmnh.harvard.edu/dimetrodon)                                                                               |
| Allosaurus       | Listed as appearing only in the pilot       | Include as a clearly marked pilot entry if the collection covers the whole show's creature checklist; do not imply a confirmed Netflix-season appearance.                                                                                                                                               |
| Baculites        | Listed without a pilot-only qualifier       | An ammonite cephalopod, not a dinosaur. Distinguish its shell from the coiled ammonite shorthand used in older period artwork. [Cretaceous Atlas classification and specimen](https://www.cretaceousatlas.org/species/baculites-baculus/)                                                               |
| Otodus megalodon | Listed without a pilot-only qualifier       | An extinct shark, not a dinosaur. It lived much later than non-avian dinosaurs, so it cannot use the existing Jurassic/Cretaceous period clues. [Natural History Museum](https://www.nhm.ac.uk/discover/megalodon--the-truth-about-the-largest-shark-that-ever-lived.html)                              |
| Tetrapodophis    | Listed as a limbed snake                    | Treat the cartoon match as provisional. Its identification as a four-legged snake has been challenged in favor of a marine lizard; avoid teaching that interpretation as established fact. [University of Alberta research summary](https://ssl.eas.ualberta.ca/cms/download/file/papers/paper_525.pdf) |
| Donnie           | Explicitly listed as an unidentified mammal | Keep the character name and uncertainty. Do not assign an invented real species, geological period, diet or scientifically authoritative reconstruction.                                                                                                                                                |

Already implemented: Ankylosaurus, Brontosaurus, Pachycephalosaurus, Parasaurolophus, Pteranodon, Quetzalcoatlus, Stegosaurus, Triceratops, Tyrannosaurus rex and Velociraptor.

Completing this checklist means accounting for **15 named taxon entries plus one unidentified character**, rather than describing all 16 as dinosaurs. Existing pterosaurs also retain their proper labels.

The audit was initially documentation-only. Implementation and all five sets of artwork are now complete; see [addition notes and sources](2026-09-16-dinosaur-additions.md). Allosaurus and Tetrapodophis use explicitly qualified approximate cartoon designs. Donnie remains excluded.
