# Teenieping guessing-game research

Research draft — 6 September 2026; extended 9 September 2026. Covers **all 86 portrait assets** in `src/assets/teenie`, including alternate forms and Romi's temporary Teenieping form. The six castle images are scenery; the global/reward Teenieping icons are not additional characters. The catalog now powers the third collection in the Animals activity. See [clue artwork](teenieping-artwork.html).

## Proposed four clue categories

| Category | Child's question        | What goes in the card                                                                |
| -------- | ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| LOOKS    | What does it look like? | One recognisable combination of hair, clothing or markings from the actual portrait. |
| PROP     | What does it use?       | Its characteristic tool, accessory or body feature used for magic.                   |
| THEME    | What is it about?       | A simple emotion, activity or motif: love, music, washing, speed.                    |
| MAGIC    | What magic happens?     | One distinctive effect, expressed as a short action.                                 |

These categories are a proposal for the existing four-card guessing format. THEME is deliberately child-friendly editorial wording, **not an exact transcription of the wiki's Concept field**. For instance, “Keep trying” simplifies Hopping's overcoming/mettle theme. LOOKS comes from the local artwork. PROP and MAGIC come from the individual linked character articles. A prop need not be visible in this particular pose.

The franchise's [SAMG listing on KOCCA](https://welcon.kocca.kr/en/directory/content/catch-teenieping-fairies-of-emotion--3803) describes Teeniepings as fairies of human emotions and behaviours. The [character index](https://catchteenieping.fandom.com/wiki/List_of_Teeniepings) and individual articles provide the detailed reference material. Direct Fandom page fetching was blocked, so those articles were consulted through their search-indexed text. The detailed facts are fan-wiki sourced, not all independently confirmed against episodes. English names follow that wiki; later-season localization and concept translations can vary. Fanon/crossover wikis and speculative discussion posts were excluded.

## Form, naming and gameplay notes

- **Bellaping (`bella`)**: Shrinks objects; Ellaping enlarges them. The sisters share a harmony theme. [Source](https://catchteenieping.fandom.com/wiki/Bellaping)
- **Chocoping (`choco`)**: Her hair itself is listed as her magical prop; do not invent a handheld chocolate tool. [Source](https://catchteenieping.fandom.com/wiki/Chocoping)
- **Cupping (`cup`)**: The pacifier-based baby spell is shared with twin Muffping, who has no portrait in this folder. [Source](https://catchteenieping.fandom.com/wiki/Cupping)
- **Cureping (`cure`)**: Selected ability is summoning helpers; ordinary medical supplies in her kit are not all magical. [Source](https://catchteenieping.fandom.com/wiki/Cureping)
- **Dadaping (`dada`)**: The current wiki uses 'rightness'; it notes older use of 'honesty'. 'Doing things right' avoids conflating him with Trueping. [Source](https://catchteenieping.fandom.com/wiki/Dadaping)
- **Ellaping (`ella`)**: Enlarges objects; Bellaping shrinks them. The sisters share a harmony theme. [Source](https://catchteenieping.fandom.com/wiki/Ellaping)
- **Flitterping (`flitter`)**: Her wings themselves are her prop; this is not a missing handheld item. [Source](https://catchteenieping.fandom.com/wiki/Flitterping)
- **Frogping (`frog`)**: Frogping is revealed as a form of Henryping. Retain both asset rows, but avoid treating them as two unrelated characters. [Source](https://catchteenieping.fandom.com/wiki/Frogping)
- **Giggleping (`giggle`)**: The transformed, mischievous counterpart of Happying; retain both pictures as distinct forms, not distinct people. [Source](https://catchteenieping.fandom.com/wiki/Giggleping)
- **Graceping (`grace`)**: Theme is a child-facing description of her graceful movement, not a claim about a fixed English translation of her concept. [Source](https://catchteenieping.fandom.com/wiki/Graceping)
- **Happying (`happy`)**: Same underlying character as Giggleping. This picture is Happying's regular royal design. [Source](https://catchteenieping.fandom.com/wiki/Happying)
- **Heartsping (`heart`)**: Use the season-one mirror design shown in this asset. Later seasons use different props and powers. [Source](https://catchteenieping.fandom.com/wiki/Heartsping)
- **Henryping (`henry`)**: Same character as Frogping in a different form; avoid placing both forms together in beginner rounds. [Source](https://catchteenieping.fandom.com/wiki/Henryping)
- **Hopping (`hop`)**: Sources vary between 'mettle' and 'overcoming'; 'Keep trying' is the editorial child-facing theme. [Source](https://catchteenieping.fandom.com/wiki/Hopping)
- **Kissyping (`kissy`)**: The stroller is listed as her prop, but her healing magic is performed by blowing kisses. [Source](https://catchteenieping.fandom.com/wiki/Kissyping)
- **Onlyping (`only`)**: This portrait matches the earlier hair-stick design; later appearances use a staff. Onlyping also appears disguised as Maskping. [Source](https://catchteenieping.fandom.com/wiki/Onlyping)
- **Romi (Romiping form) (`romi`)**: Human Romi temporarily transformed into a Teenieping in season 5, not an independent emotion fairy. MAGIC describes what happened to her, not a verified innate power. Keep as an optional bonus character. No dedicated prop or innate magic for this temporary form was established by the consulted sources; do not reuse human Romi's catching tools as if they were this form's prop. [Source](https://catchteenieping.fandom.com/wiki/Romi_Became_a_Teenieping)
- **Shimmerping (`shimmer`)**: Her theme is healing, but her flute also makes ice. The ice clue is intentional and supported by the character page. [Source](https://catchteenieping.fandom.com/wiki/Shimmerping)
- **Sleeping (`sleep`)**: The wiki uses Sleeping, also known as Sleepyping; the filename is 'sleep'. [Source](https://catchteenieping.fandom.com/wiki/Sleeping)
- **Sneakyping (`sneaky`)**: Simplified description of the wiki's removal of an evil self; do not interpret this as stealing ordinary belongings. [Source](https://catchteenieping.fandom.com/wiki/Sneakyping)
- **Snuggyping (`snuggle`)**: Filename 'snuggle' maps to the wiki's Snuggyping. The sheep helpers and laundry equipment may need separate clue artwork. [Source](https://catchteenieping.fandom.com/wiki/Snuggyping)
- **Sweetping (`sweet`)**: This selected power requires Sweetping and Tangyping together; it is not exclusive to Sweetping. [Source](https://catchteenieping.fandom.com/wiki/Sweetping)
- **Tangyping (`tangy`)**: This selected power requires Tangyping and Sweetping together; it is not exclusive to Tangyping. [Source](https://catchteenieping.fandom.com/wiki/Sweetping)

## How these clues would work in a round

Start with THEME, then PROP or MAGIC, and use LOOKS as the easiest final clue. Many themes and powers overlap; choose distractors using the full four-clue set rather than assuming a single clue uniquely identifies a character. For example, Bellaping and Timidping both shrink things, but their drums/accordion and portraits separate them. Sweetping and Tangyping share cooperative powers; their staff shapes and appearances matter.

Treat the two alternate-form pairs (Happying/Giggleping and Henryping/Frogping) as related entries when choosing answers. Keep Romiping as a bonus entry: its magic row describes a transformation caused by friends, rather than inventing an innate Teenieping power. The table is fully populated, but that exception is not a production-ready ordinary magic clue.

No new clue artwork has been generated. This is the research matrix for deciding the game content; portrait matches and simpler wording should be reviewed before making the child-facing cards.
