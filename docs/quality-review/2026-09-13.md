consolidates calendar and solar logic, and simplifies ones-counter regrouping.

## Changes

- **Optimistic state:** `lib/optimisticState.ts` owns merging and settlement for
  chores, tests, and coalesced document updates. Failed writes restore only their
  matching fields, preserving newer values and other tasks. Plain state operations
  no longer live inside the React hook module. The hook's leverage-risk score
  falls from 92 to 12; its unnecessary assertions are removed.
- **Chore subscriptions:** removed unused title drafts, queued edits, individual
  creation wrappers, and completion-count output. The dashboard's active
  creation/edit path still validates and persists its settings. Chore deletion,
  activity state, resets, and star awards retain their existing behavior.
- **Test defaults:** each of the six default slots selects the first saved test
  of its type for the active child, preserving saved-test order and fallback
  behavior without a separate map-building loop.
- **Calendar:** a single grid renders dates and padding; selection/today colors
  are resolved together. Month clamping, leap years, holiday recovery, and date
  selection have regression coverage.
- **Large numbers:** bounded arithmetic replaces nested carry/borrow branches.
  Tests cover regrouping, borrowing, zero, the 19-counter limit, and the 99-total
  limit. Operand and answer grids share their layout definition.
- **Solar calculations:** one time-zone-part reader replaces repeated parsing;
  solar positioning and globe centering share longitude normalization. Direct
  wraparound tests reduce the geometry module's locality risk from 23 to 5.

Locality improvement is specifically stronger direct test evidence; historical
churn and co-change remain. Import edges increase from 989 to 992, and deep
relative imports from 138 to 139 as consumers share the new helpers. Dependency
cycles remain zero and layer violations remain 30. The remaining three clone
groups include lifecycle symmetry and small image-validation functions; they
were not forced into unrelated abstractions.
