# Teenie Ping Time Explorer icons

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool for the Clock, Weather and Globe controls. The existing calendar artwork (`src/assets/themes/teenie/calendar.webp`) was the composition/style reference for all three. Character selection uses the project's `src/data/teeniepingProfiles.json` descriptions.

## Clock — Hurryping

Asset: `src/assets/themes/teenie/clock-character.png`

Identity reference: `src/assets/teenie/hurry.webp`

Prompt:

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: one tiny icon-only children's app navigation button. Generate ONE square icon on a truly transparent background with clean alpha edges. Input image 1 is the exact character identity reference. Input image 2 is the existing calendar button, a style and composition reference: match its cheerful polished colorful chibi toy illustration, crisp edges, big expressive face, character upper body behind a large clear functional prop in the foreground. Both face and prop must read instantly at 48-56 pixels. Tight framing, no more than 4% transparent outer margin. Character face fills upper half, functional prop occupies lower 40%, hands naturally holding it. Preserve the character's specific identity, colors and distinctive hairstyle/headgear. No background scene, no button frame, no text, no words, no letters, no watermark. Simplify small details. Do not copy the calendar, book, or diary from image 2. Subject: Hurryping from image 1, with blue aviator helmet and dark-blue round goggles, coral forelock, bright magenta eyes and blue outfit. Smiling, holding ONE large gold-rimmed round analogue clock in front of his chest. White face, two chunky contrasting clock hands and simple hour ticks, no numerals. The clock face must be fully visible and occupy at least 40% of the overall icon. No rocket, no speed lines.
